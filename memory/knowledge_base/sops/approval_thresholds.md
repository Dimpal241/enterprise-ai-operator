# Invoice Approval & Delegation of Authority Policy

**Document ID:** SOP-FIN-2024-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Applies To:** All Accounts Payable, Autonomous AI Operators, and Department Managers  

---

## 1. Spending Thresholds & Approval Routing

All corporate purchases, vendor invoices, and recurring subscriptions must adhere to the following financial approval tiers:

| Invoice Total ($ USD) | Required Authority | Action & Routing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$0.00 – $1,999.99** | Autonomous Operator | **AUTO_APPROVED**. Directly entered into payment batch with valid PO reference. |
| **$2,000.00 – $9,999.99** | Department Manager | **PENDING_APPROVAL**. Agent must hold payment, capture evidence, and request human review. |
| **$10,000.00+** | Chief Financial Officer (CFO) | **ESCALATED_CFO**. Mandatory dual-signature human authorization required. |

---

## 2. Purchase Order (PO) Matching Rules

1. **Two-Way Matching Requirement**: Every commercial invoice must cite a valid pre-approved Purchase Order number matching format `PO-YYYY-NNN`.
2. **Missing PO Exception**: Invoices received without a registered PO must **NOT** be paid. They must be routed to the Purchasing Department with a discrepancy flag.
3. **Variance Limits**: If the invoiced amount exceeds the authorized PO amount by more than 5%, the invoice is paused for line-item audit.

---

## 3. Autonomous AI Operator Guidelines

1. **Policy Consultation First**: Before entering or confirming any transaction on the ERP portal, the AI Operator must check the vendor status and verify spending limits.
2. **Evidence Preservation**: The AI Operator must record a full screenshot and verification audit log whenever an invoice is submitted.
3. **Human-in-the-Loop Trigger**: If the invoice amount is $2,000.00 or higher, the agent is strictly prohibited from bypassing human approval.
